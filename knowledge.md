## 1. National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF)
- **NSQF Structure**: Organized into levels from Level 1 (entry/helper level) to Level 10 (doctorate/advanced professional level).
- **NSQF Level 1 - 2**: Foundational / pre-vocational skills, school-level basic introduction.
- **NSQF Level 3 - 4**: Standard Industrial Training Institute (ITI) certificate level (1-year to 2-year CTS courses like Electrician, Fitter, Welder). Prepares candidates for skilled technician and operator roles.
- **NSQF Level 5**: Advanced certificate / Diploma entry level. Includes Crafts Instructor Training Scheme (CITS) qualifications.
- **NSQF Level 6 - 7**: Polytechnic Diploma (3 years or 2 years lateral entry) and Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc) degrees.
- **NSQF Level 8 - 10**: Postgraduate diplomas, Master of Vocation (M.Voc), and specialized research/leadership roles.
- **Vertical Mobility**: A student completing an ITI certificate (NSQF Level 4) is eligible for lateral entry into the second year of a Polytechnic Diploma (NSQF Level 5/6), and subsequently into engineering degree (B.Tech/B.E.) second year or B.Voc programs.

## 2. Training Schemes & Institutions in India
- **Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS)**: Delivered through Government and Private ITIs across India under the Directorate General of Training (DGT), Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE).
- **National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)**:
  - On-the-job training with industry partners.
  - Stipend framework: Governed by the Apprentices Act. Government provides partial stipend subsidy to employers under NAPS.
  - Duration typically ranges between 6 months to 2 years depending on trade.
- **Polytechnic Colleges**: State Board of Technical Education approved institutions offering 3-year diplomas post-Class 10 or 2-year lateral entry post-ITI / 12th Vocational.
- **PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana)**: Short-term skill training and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for school dropouts and informal workers.

## 3. Important Rules for Answering Inquiries
1. **Never quote specific salary figures** (e.g., do not say "an electrician earns Rs 25,000/month"). Instead, explain that wages depend on trade specialization, skill level, certification (NSQF), and local industrial demand in their district. Refer users to the SkillPath Portal's Recommendations screen for verified local data.
2. **Never quote specific college fees or guaranteed placement percentages**. Refer users to the official state admission portal (e.g., Maharashtra DVET / ITI Admission portal) or the portal's Recommendations tab.
3. **Neutral comparison**: Both vocational pathways (ITI/Polytechnic/B.Voc) and academic pathways (Arts/Science/Commerce/B.Sc) have distinct strengths. Academic degrees offer broad conceptual and theoretical grounding; vocational courses offer early practical hands-on skills, job readiness, and direct earning potential with lateral pathways to higher degrees.
4. **Human counsellor referral**: When a family has deep financial concerns, interpersonal stress, safety worries, or needs district-specific seat allocation guidance, encourage them to click the "Talk to a Human Counsellor" button.
